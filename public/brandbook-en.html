Visual identity

Logo & variations

The icon is an X built from two interlocking curved strokes — connection and transformation — in a purple gradient. The primary logo pairs it with the XENTRIS wordmark. Always use the original files: never redraw, distort or recolor the brand.

  • Respect the clear space: leave at least the height of the X around the logo.
  • At small sizes use only the X icon (favicon, avatar, app).
  • Over photos or busy backgrounds use the white or black monochrome version.
  • Do not apply shadows, outlines, rotations or gradients other than the original.
  • Do not alter the proportion between icon and wordmark.
  • Alternative versions: there is always a logo ready for every medium or application.

Color palette
